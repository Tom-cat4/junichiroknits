About Me

Hi, I'm Thomas!

I'm a Mechanical Engineering PhD student with a serious passion for knitting. When I'm not running CFD simulations or studying hyporheic exchange, you'll find me with two needles and a skein of something soft.

This site is where I share my knitting journey — project write-ups, pattern notes, yarn reviews, and the occasional rambling blog post about life in grad school.
